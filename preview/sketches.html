Which one feels right?

  • A
    Studio DeskClosest to real gear. You build beats one hit at a time and record your own melodies. Best if you want to learn how songs are put together.
  • B
    Loop GridFastest way to sound good. You mix ready-made loops that always fit together, like a DJ. Best for jamming and arranging.
  • C
    Chord PadsBest for writing songs. Every pad and bar is in tune, so you can't play a wrong note.
